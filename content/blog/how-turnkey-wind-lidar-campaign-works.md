# How a Turnkey Wind LiDAR Campaign Works

A **turnkey wind LiDAR campaign** means one operator owns the chain from site plan to QC’d data — so developers are not stitching together logistics, power, telecoms and data cleaning.

## Four stages

### 1. Plan
Measurement objectives, heights, duration, access, power and communications.

### 2. Deploy
Transport, install and commission the LiDAR and autonomous power system with documented handover.

### 3. Monitor
Remote monitoring of uptime, data flow and anomalies — with intervention when something drifts.

### 4. Deliver
Structured datasets, availability metrics and campaign reporting throughout the project.

## Who it is for

Onshore wind developers, owners preparing repowering, and advisors who need rotor-height measurements without building an internal measurement ops team.

# LiDAR vs Met Mast for Wind Resource Assessment

Met masts set the historical standard for wind measurement. **Vertical profiling LiDAR** now covers modern hub heights faster and with less civil works — and is widely accepted in bankable campaigns when classified, verified and properly operated.

## Met mast strengths

- Long industry track record  
- Direct cup/sonic measurements at instrumented heights  
- Strong long-term reference when well maintained  

## LiDAR strengths

- Heights matching today’s rotors without a 120–180 m tower  
- Faster deploy / relocate for prospecting or multi-point campaigns  
- Often lower cost and fewer permitting constraints than a tall mast  

## Practical strategy

Many projects combine both: mast for long-term reference, LiDAR for height coverage and spatial sampling. Favionus designs campaigns around your uncertainty targets — not a one-size device pitch.

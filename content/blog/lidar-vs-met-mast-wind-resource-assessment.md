# LiDAR vs Met Mast for Bankable Wind Resource Assessment

Met masts set the historical standard for wind measurement. **Vertical profiling LiDAR** now covers modern hub heights faster and with less civil works, and it is widely accepted in bankable campaigns when classified, verified and properly operated.

## Met mast: strengths and limits

- Long industry track record
- Direct cup/sonic measurements at instrumented heights
- Strong long-term reference when well maintained

## Vertical profiling LiDAR: strengths and limits

- Heights matching today's rotors without a 120–180 m tower
- Faster to deploy and relocate for prospecting or multi-point campaigns
- Often lower cost and fewer permitting constraints than a tall mast
- In complex terrain, a LiDAR usually still needs a mast or on-site verification plus flow modelling

## Combining LiDAR and a mast

Many projects combine both: the mast as a long-term reference, the LiDAR for height coverage and spatial sampling. Favionus designs campaigns around your uncertainty targets, not around a one-size-fits-all device.

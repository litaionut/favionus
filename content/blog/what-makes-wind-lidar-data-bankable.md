# What Makes Wind LiDAR Data “Bankable”?

Banks and advisors do not fund projects on raw sensor output alone. **Bankable wind LiDAR data** means measurements collected, verified and documented so that independent engineers can defend uncertainty assumptions in an energy yield assessment.

## Building blocks of bankability

1. **IEC-classified devices** — vertical profiling LiDARs assessed under IEC 61400-50-2 (and related guidance)  
2. **Verification** — comparison against an IEC-compliant met mast before/after campaign where required  
3. **Availability** — high data recovery with documented gaps and interventions  
4. **Quality control** — systematic flagging, filters and traceable processing  
5. **Campaign reporting** — commissioning records, events log, statistics and metadata

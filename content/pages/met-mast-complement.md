# Met Mast Complement — Rotor-Swept Wind Profiles

A met mast remains valuable for long-term reference, but hub heights keep rising. Favionus **complements met masts with vertical profiling LiDAR** so you capture the full rotor-swept wind profile and reduce shear extrapolation uncertainty.

## When to complement a mast

- Hub height exceeds practical mast height
- You need concurrent multi-height profiling for shear and veer
- Complex terrain where additional measurement points reduce uncertainty
- Verification or co-location campaigns supporting bankable assessments

## Deliverables

Quality-controlled multi-height wind speed and direction time series, availability metrics, turbulence indicators and campaign reporting aligned with your engineering workflow.

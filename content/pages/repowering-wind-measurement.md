# Repowering Wind Measurement Campaigns

Repowering replaces older turbines with taller, larger-rotor machines. Legacy mast data often stop short of new hub and tip heights. Favionus provides **repowering wind measurement campaigns** with vertical profiling LiDAR so decisions rest on current, height-appropriate data.

## What we measure for

- Wind speed and direction at modern hub and rotor heights  
- Shear and profile changes relevant to new machine layouts  
- Campaign designs sized for financing and yield re-assessment timelines  

## Campaign model

Plan → deploy → monitor → deliver QC’d data. Same turnkey operating model as our greenfield campaigns, tuned for operating sites and repowering schedules.

# Turnkey Wind LiDAR Measurement Campaigns

Favionus delivers **turnkey onshore wind LiDAR measurement campaigns**: campaign design, deployment, autonomous power, 24/7 remote monitoring and quality-controlled datasets ready for engineering and financing workflows. Based in Bucharest, campaigns across Europe.

## What a turnkey LiDAR campaign includes

- IEC-classified vertical profiling LiDAR units from leading manufacturers, matched to your site and hub height
- Documented commissioning and decommissioning
- Continuous remote monitoring of system health and data streams
- Systematic quality control and traceable campaign reporting
- Structured deliverables for energy yield and investment decisions

## Wind LiDAR rental

Prefer to run the measurement campaign yourself? Favionus also offers **wind LiDAR rental**: LiDAR units from leading manufacturers, matched to your site and hub height, for your own team or consultant to operate.

## How a campaign works

1. **Plan**: objectives, heights, duration and site requirements
2. **Deploy**: transport, installation and commissioning of the LiDAR and its autonomous power system
3. **Monitor**: continuous monitoring of performance and incoming measurements
4. **Deliver**: quality-controlled wind data and reporting throughout the project

## Use cases

- **[Wind resource assessment](/wind-resource-assessment/)**: on-site measurement for greenfield wind farm development
- **[Met mast complement](/met-mast-complement/)**: measure across the full rotor-swept height alongside an existing mast
- **[Repowering](/repowering-wind-measurement/)**: measure at modern hub and rotor heights
- **Site investigation**: characterise prospective wind farm sites quickly

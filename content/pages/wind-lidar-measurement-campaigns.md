# Wind LiDAR Measurement Campaigns

Favionus delivers **turnkey onshore wind LiDAR measurement campaigns**: campaign design, deployment, autonomous power, 24/7 remote monitoring, and quality-controlled datasets ready for engineering and financing workflows.

## What you get

- IEC-classified vertical profiling LiDAR technology
- Documented commissioning and decommissioning
- Continuous remote monitoring of system health and data streams
- Systematic quality control and traceable campaign reporting
- Structured deliverables for energy yield and investment decisions

## Typical use cases

1. **Wind resource assessment** — greenfield measurement for project development  
2. **Met mast complement** — extend measurement heights across the rotor-swept profile  
3. **Repowering** — measure at modern hub and rotor heights  
4. **Site investigation** — fast characterisation of prospective locations  

## How a campaign works

1. **Plan** — objectives, heights, duration, site requirements  
2. **Deploy** — transport, install, commission LiDAR and power  
3. **Monitor** — performance and incoming measurements  
4. **Deliver** — QC’d wind data and reporting throughout the project

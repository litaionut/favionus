# Wind Resource Assessment with LiDAR

Accurate on-site wind data underpins energy yield assessments, turbine selection and bankability. Favionus runs **LiDAR wind resource assessment campaigns** that produce finance-grade, quality-controlled measurements without the full cost and permitting burden of a tall met mast alone.

## Why LiDAR for resource assessment

- Measure at modern turbine hub and tip heights (vertical profiling)
- Faster mobilisation than a tall meteorological mast
- Suitable where tower height limits, permitting or environmental constraints apply
- Data suitable as a primary campaign or alongside existing masts

## Our approach

We define measurement objectives and heights with your wind engineers, deploy an IEC-classified LiDAR with autonomous power, monitor availability continuously, and deliver structured datasets with campaign reporting you can hand to advisors and lenders.
